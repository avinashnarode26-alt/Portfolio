SUSTAINABLE ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING

What if engineers could compare the environmental impact of different additive manufacturing processes before printing a component? This concept explores a data-driven approach to estimating carbon emissions from materials, electricity, manufacturing waste and transportation.

What does the calculation consider?

01 · Material

Raw material production, consumed material and a simplified allowance for manufacturing waste.

02 · Manufacturing

Electricity consumed during printing and post-processing, multiplied by the selected electricity carbon intensity.

03 · Transportation

A simplified estimate based on the mass transported, distance and transport emission factor.

From concept to engineering tool

The future application could extract geometry from uploaded CAD models, estimate build orientation and support material, use measured machine energy data, compare supplier locations, and produce a transparent report for each manufacturing option.

AM Sustainability — an independent engineering concept by Avinash. Demo results are illustrative and should not be used for procurement or environmental claims.
