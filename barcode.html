Scan. Connect. Return.

Demonstration QR code — not linked to a registered item or an owner's private contact details.

The problem

Losing everyday belongings is frustrating. Finding them does not always solve the problem: the person who finds an item may have no way to identify or contact its owner. Sometimes a useful item is never returned simply because the connection is missing.

The idea

Give every registered belonging a unique QR code. When someone scans it, a dedicated webpage explains how to contact the owner and return the item.

01 / IDENTIFY

A unique QR code

Place a QR label on keys, bags, luggage or other suitable belongings. Each code identifies one item.

02 / CONNECT

A simple webpage

The finder scans the code and opens an item-specific page with clear return instructions.

03 / PROTECT

Privacy by design

The owner's email address and home address stay hidden. A private messaging system can connect the finder and owner.

How it could work

1. Register

The owner registers an item and receives its unique QR code.

2. Scan

Someone finds the item and scans the QR code using a smartphone camera.

3. Reconnect

The finder sends a message through the webpage. The owner receives a notification and arranges a safe return.

The principle: Make returning a lost item easier than leaving it behind.

Where it could be used

Keys

House keys, car keys and keychains.

Bags & luggage

Backpacks, suitcases and travel bags.

Everyday belongings

Water bottles, notebooks and other personal items.

From concept to reality

The first version would demonstrate the experience. A future implementation could include item registration, unique QR-code generation, a secure database, private messaging and email notifications.

The aim is to develop a practical, privacy-conscious Lost & Found service that can grow into a real product.

Lost & Found QR — an independent concept by Avinash. This page is a concept demonstration, not yet a working item-registration or recovery service.
